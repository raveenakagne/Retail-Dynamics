# Retail Dynamics

## Retail Customer Intelligence & Sales Analytics Platform

## 1. Project Overview

Retail Dynamics is an end-to-end retail analytics project designed to transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights.

The project uses the Online Retail II dataset containing more than one million transactions from a UK-based online retailer.

The solution will combine data preparation, SQL, Python, statistical analysis, machine learning, cloud technologies, and business intelligence to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, retention, and purchasing patterns.

---

## 2. Business Problem

The retailer has a large volume of historical transaction data but lacks a consolidated analytical solution for understanding business performance and customer behavior.

Management needs reliable answers to questions such as:

* How is revenue changing over time?
* Which products and markets generate the most revenue?
* Who are the company's most valuable customers?
* How frequently do customers return?
* Which customers are becoming inactive?
* Which products are frequently purchased together?
* Can customers be grouped according to purchasing behavior?

Without a structured analytics platform, transactional data cannot easily be converted into actionable decisions for sales, marketing, product, and customer-retention teams.

---

## 3. Project Objective

Build an end-to-end Retail Customer Intelligence & Sales Analytics Platform that transforms raw transactional data into reliable business KPIs, customer segments, purchasing patterns, predictive insights, and interactive dashboards.

The solution should enable stakeholders to make data-driven decisions related to sales growth, customer retention, marketing, and product strategy.

* Revenue per Customer
* Purchase Frequency
* Repeat Customer Rate
* Cancellation / Return Rate
* Customer Recency
* Customer Frequency
* Customer Monetary Value
* Customer Retention Rate
* Customer Lifetime Value

Exact calculation rules and business definitions will be established during the requirements and data-analysis phases.

## 11. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

* Raw transaction data is transformed into a documented and validated analytical dataset.
* Core retail KPIs can be reproduced using SQL.
* Customer, sales, product, and geographic performance can be analyzed.
* Meaningful customer segments and purchasing patterns can be identified.
* Business users can explore major KPIs through an interactive Power BI dashboard.
* The analytical workflow is documented and reproducible.
* Business findings are translated into actionable recommendations.
